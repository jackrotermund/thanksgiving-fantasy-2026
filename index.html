Scoring rules (Full PPR)

  • 1 pt per reception
  • 0.1 pt per rushing yard and receiving yard
  • 6 pts per rushing or receiving TD
  • 0.04 pt per passing yard (1 per 25), 4 pts per passing TD
  • -2 per interception thrown, -2 per fumble lost
  • 2 pts per 2 point conversion (passer, rusher or receiver)

Kicker

  • FG 0 to 39 yds: 3, 40 to 49: 4, 50+: 5
  • PAT made: 1
  • Missed FG: -1, missed PAT: -1

Team defense / special teams

  • Points allowed (total opponent score): 0 = 10, 1 to 6 = 7, 7 to 13 = 4, 14 to 20 = 1, 21 to 27 = 0, 28 to 34 = -1, 35+ = -4 (applies live once the game kicks off)
